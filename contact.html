Before you message

Common questions.

What do you need from me to start a website?

Your business name, logo, photos, the text for each page and your contact details. We can't build until your content arrives, so the faster you send it, the faster you go live.

How does payment work?

For websites, 50% is paid before work begins and 50% before final handover. For laptops and other things, payment is agreed on WhatsApp.

How long does a website take?

Usually a few weeks for a full custom site, depending on how quickly your content arrives. Ready-made template sites are quicker.

Do I need to own a domain?

A domain (like yourbusiness.com.ng) makes you look professional. We can register it for you at the actual cost plus a small handling fee.

Do you sell laptops?

Yes, new or used. Message us on WhatsApp with what you want and what you need it for.
