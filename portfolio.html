About

A well of knowledge, put to work.

I'm Maxwell, a passionate frontend web developer based in Nigeria. I build fast, mobile-friendly websites by hand, in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, for businesses that want to look credible online.

My name has Scottish and Old English roots, often read as "great well": a well of knowledge. That idea is the heart of Maxwell Hub: learn something valuable, build something useful, and share it.
